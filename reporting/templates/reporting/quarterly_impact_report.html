Methodology and Interpretation

Indicators are calculated from records maintained in the AFRIVELTA WomenPregnancy Platform. Counts represent recorded activities and service outputs, not necessarily unique individuals reached.

Programme activities are grouped by last update date because the current activity model does not store a dedicated completion timestamp.

Provider verification trends use the latest recorded verification date rather than a complete historical verification event log. Referral completion trends use the recorded closure date.

Referral information is reported in aggregate. This report does not include referral notes, personal identifiers, diagnoses or detailed health records.

Recorded service outputs should not be interpreted as demonstrated improvements in pregnancy outcomes, maternal health or other population health outcomes without appropriate outcome measurements.
